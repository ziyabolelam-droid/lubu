About Lebu+

A simpler way to discover stories worth your time.

Discover with confidence

Lebu+ brings film and series discovery into one place. Browse the catalogue, check available title information, and save the stories you want to revisit.

  • Search titles by name, genre or keyword.
  • Review release details and ratings when reliable information is available.
  • Open trusted information pages and available viewing options.
  • Build a personal My List in your current browser.

Frequently asked questions

Lebu+ helps you discover titles and directs you to external information or provider pages. Whether a title can be streamed depends on the provider, your location and your subscription.

Your saved titles stay in this browser on this device. They are not synced to other devices or accounts, and clearing your browser data may remove them.

We avoid guessing ratings, release dates or plot details. When a title cannot be identified confidently, check the linked information page before relying on its details.

Use Browse to search by title or keyword, then narrow the results with the available filters. Add titles to My List to find them again easily.
